# EAS-001: Terminology

**Action** — an observable operation performed by an agent or delegated actor.

**Assessment subject** — the entity whose requirements are aggregated in one
assessment record.

**Decision** — a recorded selection among proceeding, escalating, blocking,
refusing, or inspecting.

**Evidence** — an observable record supporting or contradicting a claim.

**Material action** — an action for which at least one of the six recorded
materiality dimensions is true.

**Run** — one bounded execution of an engineering task, represented by a run
record.

**Run outcome** — `completed`, `escalated`, or `blocked`.

**Task result** — `satisfied`, `partially_satisfied`, `not_satisfied`, or
`indeterminate`.

**Verification claim** — a report entry that names a check, its status, and its
evidence references.

This vocabulary is informative in EAS 0.1. Normative obligations are contained
only in identified requirements.
